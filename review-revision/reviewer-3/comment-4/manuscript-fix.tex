Security was also machine-checked with the Tamarin prover (v1.12.0). Under a Dolev--Yao attacker with perfect symbolic cryptography, the abstract protocol satisfies identity binding (P1), authorization integrity (P2), nonce freshness (P3), non-transferability (P4), and delegated-predicate soundness (P5); \texttt{exists-trace} lemmas confirm that honest executions exist, so these results are not vacuous. Ablations that remove the cross-layer binding or the on-chain guard admit concrete counterexamples for P4 and P5, which motivates those components. A separate ProVerif cross-check confirms that every settlement is preceded by an authorization event; the stronger injective correspondence fails as expected because off-chain messages alone cannot prevent replay, which is why nonce consumption is enforced on-chain. These proofs concern the abstract protocol logic rather than a line-by-line correctness proof of the TypeScript and Solidity implementation; code-level agreement is checked separately by commitment-parity tests and the deterministic verifier.
